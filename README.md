# DRIP BY A²

**Modern essentials. Strong colors. Clean silhouettes. Everyday statement pieces.**

A custom-built online store and admin panel for **DRIP BY A²**, a fashion brand for boys and girls in Bangladesh.

## Highlights

- **A bold, animated storefront**: scroll-story hero, 3D product carousels, lookbook, story chapters, and a separate **Boys** (blue) and **Girls** (pink) side.
- **English and বাংলা** everywhere, switched with one tap.
- **Customer accounts**: sign in with email or **Google**, a personal customer ID, order history with live status, saved addresses, and a wishlist on every device. Guests can still buy without an account.
- **Checkout made for Bangladesh**: cash on delivery, bKash / Nagad / Rocket, SSLCommerz and bKash gateways, delivery areas, and coupons.
- **Printable invoices** (print or save as PDF) for customers and the shop.
- **After the sale**: order tracking, returns and exchanges, made-to-measure requests, and reviews.
- **An admin panel for everything**: homepage builder with live preview and per-screen photo framing, products with size × colour variants and SKUs, inventory history, orders, customers, a payments ledger, a media library that accepts iPhone photos, and settings for every text and colour.
- **Fast pages**: photos are optimised automatically, with no heavy framework.
